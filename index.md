## 🎯 What Is This Tool?

**dynamics-365-power-pane-next** is a small but powerful helper that lives right inside your web browser. It adds a handy panel to Microsoft Dynamics 365 (also known as Dataverse) when you're using it online. Think of it like a Swiss Army knife for people who work with Dynamics 365 every day.

This tool helps you in three main ways:

1. **In-page action pane** – Quick buttons and shortcuts appear right on your screen so you can do common tasks without hunting through menus.
2. **Diagnostics** – It can show you helpful technical details about what's happening behind the scenes, which is great when something isn't working right.
3. **Real user impersonation** – This lets you temporarily see Dynamics 365 the way another user sees it, which is super helpful for testing or troubleshooting.

The best part? You don't need to be a programmer to use it. If you can click a button, you can use this tool.

## 🔧 How to Use It

Once installed, the extension works automatically when you open Dynamics 365 in your browser. Here's what you'll see:

### The Action Pane

Look for a small icon in your browser's toolbar area (top right corner). Click it, and a panel will slide out. This panel gives you:

- **Quick actions** – Buttons for common tasks like refreshing, opening settings, or jumping to specific areas
- **Context tools** – Tools that change based on what you're viewing in Dynamics 365
- **Shortcut keys** – Keyboard shortcuts you can use to speed up your work

### Diagnostics

If you ever need to check what's happening with your Dynamics 365 page:

1. Click the extension icon
2. Look for the "Diagnostics" or "Info" button
3. A new panel will show you technical details like page load time, errors, and system information

This is incredibly helpful when you're working with support teams or trying to figure out why something isn't working.

### Impersonation

This feature lets you view Dynamics 365 as if you were another user:

1. Click the extension icon
2. Find the "Impersonate User" option
3. Enter or select the user you want to view as
4. Click "Start Impersonation"

You'll now see Dynamics 365 the way that user sees it. When you're done, just click "Stop Impersonation" to return to your normal view.

> **Note:** Impersonation requires special permissions in Dynamics 365. If you don't see this option, your administrator may have restricted it.

## ❓ Troubleshooting

### "The extension won't install"

- Make sure you've downloaded the complete file
- Check that you have Developer Mode turned on in your browser
- Try restarting your browser and trying again

### "I don't see the extension icon"

- Click the puzzle piece icon in your browser toolbar (this shows hidden extensions)
- Pin the extension so it stays visible
- Make sure the extension is enabled in your browser's extension list

### "The panel doesn't appear on my Dynamics 365 page"

- Refresh your Dynamics 365 page
- Make sure you're on a model-driven app page (custom pages may not support it)
- Check that the extension is enabled

### "I can't use impersonation"

- This feature requires admin permissions in Dynamics 365
- Contact your system administrator to get the proper access
